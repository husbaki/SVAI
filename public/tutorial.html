Manage your sources

Open Sources to upload files, create a document, or edit an existing record. Search refreshes when documents change.

Inspect the results

Results shows recorded evaluation scores, errors and estimated read-only inference costs. These measurements come from the saved experiment. Training and document-update costs are excluded from the serving-cost charts.

Optional details

Technical details shows the underlying output and execution mode. Advanced options lets you select a mode or compare live responses.
